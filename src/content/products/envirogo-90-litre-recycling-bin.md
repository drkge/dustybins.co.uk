The EnviroGo recycling bin is perfect for tight spaces or frequent relocation. Available in various types for different recycling needs, it features a large opening, a recess for labels, and a slim design for easy use in corridors. With a front handle and non-marking wheels, it's also easy to maneuver. Plus, its tilting sack frame allows for quick and safe maintenance.

Does not include A3 poster.
