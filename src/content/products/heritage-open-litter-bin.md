## Heritage Open Bin: Classic Design, Simple Functionality

The Heritage Open Bin offers a timeless aesthetic with user-friendly features.

- **Enduring Style:** This bin boasts a classic look that complements a variety of environments.
- **Effortless Disposal:** The open design allows for easy "walk-by" waste disposal, promoting convenience.
- **Secure Closure:** The bin features a secure key lock to prevent tampering and keep waste contained.
- **Easy Maintenance:** The lift-off lid provides full access to the liner for hassle-free emptying.
- **Stable and Secure:** The integrated ballast cartridge ensures stability, even in windy conditions. (Ballast, such as sand or gravel, may need to be purchased separately).
- **Elegant Touch:** The gold banding adds a touch of sophistication (availability may vary).
