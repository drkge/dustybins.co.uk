**Charming and Compact: The Mini Classic Litter Bin**

This mini version of our popular Classic Litter Bin offers the same timeless style in a space-saving size, ideal for smaller outdoor areas. Here's what makes it a mini marvel:

- **Easy Access, Big Impact:** The double aperture design allows for convenient "walk-by" disposal, encouraging proper waste management in a compact footprint.
- **Hygiene Hero:** The anti-fly poster dimpled surface helps deter pesky insects, promoting cleanliness.
- **Personalised Champion:** Two panels offer ample space for personalized labels, making waste sorting clear and efficient even on a smaller scale.
- **Simple Upkeep:** The lift-off lid provides easy, all-around access to the liner for effortless maintenance.
- **Stylish Touch:** A decorative gold band adds a touch of sophistication to the bin's design.
- **Stable Foundation:** The plinth features molded-in stiffening ribs, eliminating the need for ground anchor plates for a secure installation.
