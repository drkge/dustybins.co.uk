The Metro Hooded Bin is your perfect solution for waste management in compact areas. This modern bin boasts a space-saving 470mm footprint without compromising on functionality:

- **Effortless Disposal:** Double apertures allow for convenient "walk-by" access, encouraging proper waste disposal.
- **Built to Endure:** The enhanced double-walled hood provides exceptional rigidity and durability, ensuring the bin can withstand daily use and harsh weather.
- **Precise Placement:** The molded aperture directs waste directly into the liner, minimizing spills and messes.
- **Enhanced Security:** Tamper-resistant plunge locking keeps contents secure and discourages misuse.
- **Easy Maintenance:** The lift-off lid offers easy, all-around access for effortless liner changes.
