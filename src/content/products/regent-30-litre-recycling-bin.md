**The Regent 30: Your Versatile Ally in Waste Management**

The Regent 30 is a tough and adaptable litter bin, perfect for a variety of locations. This practical bin offers:

- **Mounting Flexibility:** Choose between wall-mounted or post-mounted installation to suit your space and needs.
- **Secure Waste Storage:** A standard sack retention belt keeps trash bags in place, preventing messes and overflows.
- **Optional Locking Lid (Sold Separately):** Upgrade to a lockable, 30-liter zinc-coated liner for added security and discretion in sensitive areas (mention if the lock is included with the liner).
