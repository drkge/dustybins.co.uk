**Looking for tough, customizable waste containers?** We offer a wide range of sizes to meet your needs, from 500 liters to 1280 liters. Our containers are built to last, with strong galvanized steel bodies and the option for a lightweight steel lid with a safety edge. You can also choose from a variety of colors to match your requirements.

**Key Features:**

- **Fire-Safe Construction:** This emphasizes the container's ability to resist flames.
- **Galvanized Steel for Durability:** This highlights the material's strength and resistance to rust.
- **Safety Edge Lid Design:** This focuses on the flattened Dutch bend's safety features.
- **Vermin-Proof Design:** This clearly states the container's resistance to pests.
- **EN840 Certified:** This concisely conveys compliance with the European standard.

**Want even more features?** We offer extras like DIN lifting points, RFID tagging, a foot-operated lid system.
