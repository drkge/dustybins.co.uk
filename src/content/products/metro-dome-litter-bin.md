**Metro Dome Bin: Modern Style Meets Compact Efficiency**

**Small Space, Big Convenience:**

The Metro Dome Bin is the perfect solution for stylish waste management in space-restricted areas. This modern bin boasts a compact 470mm footprint without compromising on functionality:

- **Effortless Disposal:** Double apertures allow for convenient "walk-by" access, encouraging proper waste disposal.
- **Secured and Sanitary:** Tamper-resistant plunge locking keeps contents secure and discourages littering.
- **Easy Maintenance:** The lift-off lid offers easy, all-around access for effortless liner changes.
- **Colorful Choice:** Add a splash of personality with colored lid options (lids sold separately) to complement your décor or promote waste sorting initiatives.
