## Heritage Slimline Bin: Saving Space, Simplifying Waste Management

The Heritage Slimline Bin prioritizes space efficiency and ergonomic use.

- **Space-Saving Design:** This bin's slim profile makes it ideal for areas with limited floor space.
- **Effortless Waste Disposal:** The front-opening door with a hinge allows for easy access to the liner, eliminating the need for lifting.
- **Cleanliness Champion:** The double aperture facilitates "walk-by" disposal, while the curved hood discourages placing waste on top. An anti-fly poster ribbed surface further deters unwanted additions.
- **Durable & Secure:** The roto-molded construction ensures strength and longevity. The slam-shut door with a multi-point locking system (including an optional quarter-turn lock) keeps waste contained and prevents tampering.
- **Convenient Features:** This bin boasts a 72-liter liner capacity, a stainless steel cigarette stubber plate (with an optional top ashtray), and a deep molded plinth for ballasting (weights sold separately). It also features pre-drilled holes for easy surface mounting.
