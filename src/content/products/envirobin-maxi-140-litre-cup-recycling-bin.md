## Envirobin Maxi Cup Recycling Bin: High-Capacity Coffee Cup Champion

The Envirobin Maxi Cup Recycling Bin is a powerhouse for handling large volumes of coffee cup waste in any environment.

- **Mega Capacity:** The 140-liter capacity tackles significant amounts of coffee cups, ideal for busy locations or centralized collection points.
- **Flexible Placement:** Use it individually or link multiple units for even greater capacity needs.
- **Effortless Maintenance:** The slam shut door with secure latch points allows for easy emptying and cleaning.
- **Built to Last:** The robust double-walled structure and molded bolt-down points ensure exceptional durability, indoors or outdoors (suitable for both internal and external use).
- **Streamlined Recycling:** The large capacity and easy emptying features make recycling coffee cups a breeze.
