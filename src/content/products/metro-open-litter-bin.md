**Metro Open Bin: Modern Style, Compact Solution**

**Small Footprint, Big Impact:**

The Metro Open Bin brings modern design to waste management in space-restricted areas. Its compact 470mm footprint makes it ideal for lobbies, offices, or anywhere a larger bin isn't feasible.
