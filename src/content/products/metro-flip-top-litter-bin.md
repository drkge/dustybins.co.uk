**Metro Flip-Top Bin: Modern Design for Discreet Waste Management**

**Small Footprint, Big on Cleanliness:**

The Metro Flip-Top Bin offers a modern solution for waste management in compact areas. Its space-saving 470mm footprint makes it ideal for lobbies, offices, or anywhere a larger bin isn't feasible. Here's what makes it a discreet and user-friendly choice:

- **Modern Design:** The sleek aesthetic complements various interior decors.
- **Hidden Waste:** The flip-top keeps trash out of sight, promoting a cleaner and more pleasant environment.
- **Discourages Wildlife:** The enclosed design helps deter pests and wildlife from accessing waste.
- **Enhanced Security:** Tamper-resistant plunge locking keeps contents secure and discourages misuse.
- **Easy Maintenance:** The lift-off lid provides easy, all-around access for effortless liner changes.
