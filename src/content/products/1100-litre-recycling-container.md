**Highly Durable Recycling Containers**

Our tough recycling containers are built to last, with capacities ranging from 500 to 1280 litres. Made from galvanized steel for maximum rust protection, they're ideal for use with comb lift vehicles.

**Choose Your Lid Configuration**

For easy recycling, the containers feature a slot entry. You can choose from a simple flap closure, or an option with a brush to prevent unwanted materials. A flap is also available with the brush guard for additional control.
