**The Consort: Your All-Around Champion for Effortless Waste Management**

The Consort is a perennial favorite for a reason. Designed and manufactured by Leafield Environmental, this bin is a versatile workhorse for various indoor and outdoor locations.

**Built to Please, Built to Last:**

- **Universal Appeal:** The Consort seamlessly integrates into any environment, from offices and lobbies to parks and playgrounds.
- **Capacity for Convenience:** Available in sizes ranging from 85 to 100 liters, you can find the perfect fit for your waste management needs.
- **Effortless Disposal:** The double aperture design allows for easy "walk-by" access, encouraging proper waste disposal.
- **Rock-Solid Construction:** The strong and durable "all in one body" moulding ensures the Consort can handle daily use with ease.
- **Simple Upkeep:** The lift-off lid provides easy, all-around access for hassle-free liner changes.
