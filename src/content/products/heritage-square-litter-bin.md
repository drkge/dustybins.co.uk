## Heritage Square Bin: Timeless Design, Modern Functionality

The Heritage Square Bin, a popular choice for its classic aesthetics, offers a winning combination of style and functionality.

- **Enduring Style:** This bin complements any environment with its traditional design.
- **Personalized Touch:** Showcase your branding or messaging with optional signage for a customized look (sold separately).
- **Spacious Capacity:** The 130-liter bin volume holds a generous 110-liter liner, ideal for moderate-traffic areas.
- **Easy Access:** Four conveniently located apertures allow for waste disposal from all sides.
- **Strength & Security:** The enhanced double wall structure ensures superior durability, while the slam-shut door with a four-point latch keeps waste contained.
- **Cleanliness Champion:** The anti-fly poster ribbed surface deters unwanted additions, and the diagonal hinged door allows for effortless access, eliminating the need for lifting.
