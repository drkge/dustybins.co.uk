**Triple the Recycling Power in a Compact Footprint:** The EnviroStack Crisp Packet Recycling Bin 96 by Leafield Environmental offers a unique solution for multi-waste recycling in tight spaces. This innovative bin combines three 32-liter units, allowing you to sort and recycle different waste streams efficiently.

- **Space-Saving Design:** The slim and compact design takes up minimal floor space, making it ideal for offices, break rooms, or anywhere with limited room.
- **Multi-Waste Ready:** Sort and recycle a variety of waste items with its three compartments.
- **Flexible Use:** Use the bin individually or connect multiple units for a larger recycling system.
- **Easy Emptying and Cleaning:** The base tipping recesses allow for effortless emptying, while the sack retention system ensures a clean and discreet experience (the bin can also be used bag-less).
