**Multi-Bin Performance in a Compact Design: The Meridian 110 Triple Liquids Bin**

Leafield Environmental's Meridian 110 Triple Liquids bin tackles all your office recycling needs in one space-saving solution.

- **Triple Threat Recycling:** Effortlessly sort your waste with dedicated compartments for 33 liters and 44 liters of dry recyclables, along with a separate 9.5-liter section for liquid waste.
- **Fits Seamlessly:** The narrow footprint allows for convenient placement in corridors or tight areas without compromising access to recycling options.
- **Promotes Cleanliness & Sustainability:** This versatile bin encourages proper waste disposal and helps maintain a clean and eco-friendly work environment.
