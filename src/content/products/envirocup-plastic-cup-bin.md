**Envirocup: Maximize Recycling Efficiency, Minimize Fuss**

Envirocup collection bins are designed to streamline your cup recycling process.

- **Smart Stacking Design:** Cups nest neatly inside each other, maximizing bin volume and reducing the need for frequent emptying. This space-saving design is perfect for any office or break room.
- **Versatile Recycling:** These bins accept both plastic cups, making them a convenient one-stop solution for your cup waste.
- **Leak-Proof and Easy to Clean:** The integrated 7.5-liter reservoir captures liquid waste, while the removable design allows for effortless cleaning.
- **Hassle-Free Cup Collection:** The circular apertures and bag-free collection tubes ensure cups drop and stack easily, preventing blockages and overflows.
