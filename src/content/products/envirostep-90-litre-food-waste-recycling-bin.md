**Maintain Peak Hygiene in Food Prep Areas:** The EnviroStep Single Food Waste Recycling Bin is specifically designed for kitchens, washrooms, catering areas, and food halls, where cleanliness is paramount.

- **Effortless Maneuvering:** The front-mounted handle and non-marking wheels allow for effortless movement, even when full.
- **Quick & Easy Maintenance:** The tilting sack frame allows for swift and secure bin cleaning in just one motion. A leak-proof drip tray catches any spills or bag leaks for added peace of mind.
- **Simple Deep Cleaning:** Minimal components enable complete disassembly for thorough cleaning in under 8 minutes.
