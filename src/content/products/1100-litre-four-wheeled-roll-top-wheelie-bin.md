## Built for Efficiency and Sustainability: CONTENUR Containers

CONTENUR containers prioritize both user comfort and visual appeal with their ergonomic design and aesthetic features. These robust bins are manufactured from high-density polyethylene, a solid-colored material resistant to water and UV damage, using an eco-friendly and recyclable approach. Furthermore, the pigments used contain no heavy metals, ensuring safety and environmental responsibility.

**Features:**

- Ergonomic and aesthetically pleasing rear-loading design.
- Manufactured from injection-molded, solid-colored high-density polyethylene, stabilized against UV radiation and water damage.
- Made with environmentally friendly, recyclable materials.
- Pigments used are free of heavy metals.

**Standards:**

- CE Marking with sound power level of 91 dB, complying with European Directive 2000/14/EC.
- UN certified for transporting hazardous materials.
- Product Approval Certificate awarded by TÜV Product Service GmbH according to European Standard EN 840-2: 2020.
