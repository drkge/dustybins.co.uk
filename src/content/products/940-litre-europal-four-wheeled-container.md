Designed for high-rise buildings, Storm's EuroPal 720 and 940 containers are ideal for waste collection via chute systems. These lightweight, galvanized steel bins are built to last, with an optional lid for use in other locations. Compatible with all major comb lift vehicles in the UK, EuroPal containers offer a convenient and durable solution for waste management.

This version emphasizes:
