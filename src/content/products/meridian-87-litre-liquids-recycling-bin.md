**Triple Threat Recycling: The Meridian 87**

Leafield Environmental's Meridian 87 Triple Liquids bin is a multi-tasking marvel for streamlined office recycling.

- **Three Stream Champion:** This bin sorts your waste into three categories: 26 liters for designated dry waste, 35 liters for another type of dry waste, and 7.5 liters for liquid waste, all in one convenient unit.
- **Space-Saving Efficiency:** The compact design with a narrow footprint allows for comfortable placement in corridors or other tight spaces, ensuring easy access for recycling without sacrificing valuable floor space.
