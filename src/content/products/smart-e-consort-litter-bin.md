**The Eco-Smart Choice: Smart E-Consort Litter Bin**

The Smart E-Consort bin combines innovation with environmental responsibility. Designed and manufactured by Leafield Environmental, this bin boasts several features that make it a smart choice:

- **Crafted from Recycled Champions:** Made from 100% recycled plastic, including chipped materials from end-of-life bins. The unique mottled appearance is a badge of its eco-friendly heritage (color may vary).
- **Sized for Convenience:** Available in capacities ranging from 85 to 100 liters, you can find the perfect fit for your location.
- **Universal Appeal:** At home indoors or outdoors, this versatile bin complements any environment.
- **Effortless Disposal:** The double aperture design allows for easy "walk-by" access, encouraging proper waste disposal.
- **Built to Last:** The strong and durable "all in one body" moulding ensures the Smart E-Consort can handle daily use with ease.
- **Enhanced Security:** The new tamper-resistant plunge lock keeps contents secure and discourages misuse.
- **Easy Maintenance:** The lift-off lid provides simple, all-around access for effortless liner changes.

**Colours may vary.**
