## Heritage Square XL Bin: Big Capacity, Classic Style

The Heritage Square XL Bin offers a generous 240-liter capacity in a timeless design. Here's what makes it ideal for busy areas:

- **Spacious Design:** Accommodates large volumes of waste, perfect for high-traffic locations or remote areas.
- **Built to Last:** The enhanced double wall structure ensures exceptional strength and durability, even in harsh coastal environments.
- **Easy Access:** Four conveniently placed apertures allow for waste disposal from any direction.
- **Secure and Sanitary:** The slam-shut door features a multi-point locking system to prevent tampering and keep waste contained. An anti-fly poster ribbed surface discourages unwanted additions. The curved hood prevents waste from being left on top.
