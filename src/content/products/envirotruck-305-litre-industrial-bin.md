Our Envirotruck 305 is a heavyweight champion when it comes to industrial recycling and general waste. This commercial-grade bin is ideal for manufacturers and businesses that generate a high volume of waste:

- **Effortless Handling:** Convenient easy-grab edges make lifting and transporting the bin a smooth operation.
- **Enhanced Mobility:** The Envirotruck 305 comes with four standard casters for effortless maneuvering around your workspace.
