## CONTENUR Rear-Loading Containers: Designed for Usability, Durability, and Sustainability

CONTENUR rear-loading containers are built to impress. They combine functionality with aesthetics, making them not only easy and comfortable to use but also a visually appealing addition to any environment. But these containers go beyond good looks. They're constructed with long-lasting materials and a commitment to the environment. Here's a closer look at the features and certifications that make CONTENUR rear-loading containers a standout choice.

**Features:**

- **Ergonomic and Aesthetic Design:** CONTENUR containers with rear-loading design prioritize both user comfort and visual appeal in every component.
- **Durable and Sustainable Materials:** Manufactured using solid-colored, high-density polyethylene (HDPE) through an injection molding process. This HDPE is UV and water stabilized for extended lifespan.
- **Eco-Friendly Construction:** Made with recyclable and environmentally friendly materials.
- **Safe Pigments:** No heavy metals are used in the pigments, ensuring safety and environmental responsibility.

**Standards:**

- **CE Marking:** Certified with sound power level following European Directive 2000/14/EC. (Noise level: 79 dB)
- **Product Approval:** TÜV Product Service GmbH certification according to European Standard EN 840-1:2020.
- **Additional Approval:** Meets specifications of RAL-GZ 951/1:2021.
