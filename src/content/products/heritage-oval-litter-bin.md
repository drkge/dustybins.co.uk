## Heritage Oval Litter Bin: Designed for Ease and Hygiene

The Heritage Oval, Leafield Environmental's latest innovation, is a front-door opening bin focused on user comfort and hygiene. Here's what makes it stand out:

- **Effortless Disposal:** The double opening allows for easy "walk-by" access, eliminating the need to stop and aim.
- **Strong & Long-Lasting:** roto-molded construction ensures the bin is both durable and built to endure.
- **Cleanliness Champion:** The curved hood discourages placing waste on top, while the anti-fly poster ribbed surface deters unwanted flyposting.
- **Easy on the Back:** The front-opening door with a diagonal hinge allows for effortless access to the liner, requiring no lifting.
- **Secure & Sanitary:** The slam-shut door with a multi-point locking system (including an optional quarter-turn lock) keeps waste contained and prevents unwanted access.
