The Envirocup Grande Cup Recycling Bin helps to increase recycling rates in offices and busy public spaces. Its unique design features a distinctive 'cup' shape and easily stackable collection tubes, making it ideal for collecting and recycling paper and plastic cups. With an integral reservoir for liquid waste and easy cleaning options, this bin is a practical and efficient solution for any waste stream.
