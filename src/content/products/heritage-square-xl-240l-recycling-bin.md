The Heritage Square recycling bin range is expertly designed to enhance traditional environments. Its black body with gold banding adds an elegant touch, while the propeller-shaped aperture insert allows for easy collection of mixed recyclable waste. You can also convert existing models from litter to recycling units by simply retro-fitting the aperture insert.
