**Double Down on Coffee Cup Recycling:**

The Envirobin Hub 143 Cup Recycling Bin offers a comprehensive solution for managing coffee cup waste in one convenient unit.

- **Separate Compartments:** This bin features a dedicated 43-liter Enviropod for collecting cup lids and a spacious 100-liter EnvirocupXL for collecting cups themselves.
- **Efficient Cup Collection:** The EnvirocupXL boasts a 7.5-liter liquid capacity and can hold up to 500 cups with a 103mm diameter, ideal for busy areas.
- **Easy Assembly:** A fixing kit is included for a simple setup process (some self-assembly required).
