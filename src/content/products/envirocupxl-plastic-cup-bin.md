**EnvirocupXL: Streamline Cup Recycling and Maximize Efficiency**

The EnvirocupXL is a champion of space-saving cup recycling! This bin utilizes a clever nesting design that allows cups to fit neatly within each other.

- **Reduced Emptying Frequency:** The efficient design maximizes bin volume, so you'll need to empty it less often, saving you time and effort.
- **Perfect for Plastic Cups:** This versatile bin accepts plastic cups, ideal for consolidated cup waste collection in your office or break room.
- **Leak-Proof with Easy Cleaning:** The integrated 7.5-liter reservoir features a molded tear-drop symbol for easy identification and captures any leftover liquids. The removable design ensures effortless cleaning.
- **Effortless Cup Collection:** The circular openings and bag-free collection tubes allow cups to drop and stack seamlessly, preventing blockages and overflows.
