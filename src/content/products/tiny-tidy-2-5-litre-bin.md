Introducing Tiny-Tidy, the compact and personal desktop unit that reduces clutter by replacing individual waste bins.

With an open aperture suitable for multiple waste streams, this unit encourages the collection of recyclables for easy disposal in larger bins. Its uniformed wall thickness and durable tray keep the unit strong for long-lasting use.
