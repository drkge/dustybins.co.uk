**The Eco-Smart Choice for Tight Spaces: Smart E-Metro Bin**

**Sustainable Style in a Compact Footprint:**

The Smart E-Metro bin is a champion for the environment, perfectly sized for space-conscious locations. This innovative bin boasts several features that make it a smart choice:

- **Crafted from Recycled Champions:** Made from 100% recycled plastic, including chipped materials from end-of-life bins. The unique mottled appearance is a badge of its eco-friendly heritage (color may vary).
- **Modern Design:** The sleek aesthetic complements various interior decors.
- **Space-Saving Efficiency:** The compact 470mm footprint makes it ideal for lobbies, offices, or anywhere a larger bin isn't an option.
- **Effortless Disposal:** The open-top design allows for easy, all-around access, encouraging proper waste disposal.
