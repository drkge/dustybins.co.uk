**Inclusive Recycling: Storm Unveils Accessible Waste Container**

The East Access Container makes recycling easier than ever for everyone! This innovative bin is perfect for wheelchair users, featuring a conveniently sized front aperture for effortless waste deposit.

**Available in 1280 and 1100 litre capacities**, the container caters to a variety of recycling needs. And for maximum convenience, it's designed based on the popular reverse lid bin. This means users can also recycle through the top lid, ensuring accessibility for all.
