**Keep Winter at Bay: Durable Grit Bins for All Seasons**

Our external grit bins come in two sizes – 200 liters and 400 liters – offering the perfect solution for storing grit, salt, or even general outdoor equipment. Built to last, these bins boast a range of features that make them a smart investment:

- **Extreme Durability:** Withstand harsh weather conditions and heavy loads, keeping contents safe and secure.
- **Extra-Strong Lid:** The double-walled lid ensures a tight seal, protecting your supplies from rain, snow, and wind.
- **Prevents Spills:** The molded-in "grit/salt" sign clearly identifies the bin's contents.
- **Space-Saving Design:** The stackable design allows for efficient storage when not in use.
- **Easy Maneuverability:** Forklift spaces integrated into the bin base allow for effortless transportation.
- **Customization Ready:** Personalize your bins with a designated label recess
