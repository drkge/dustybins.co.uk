**Octaplus Maxi: The Heavy-Duty Champion for Busy Locations**

Leafield Environmental's Octaplus Maxi is a hard-wearing litter bin built to handle the toughest environments, like schools and garage forecourts. Here's why it's a winning choice:

- **Built to Last:** This durable bin can withstand daily wear and tear, ensuring a long lifespan.
- **Universal Appeal:** Suitable for both indoor and outdoor use, making it a versatile waste management solution.
- **Easy Waste Disposal:** Four-way apertures allow users to discard waste from any direction, promoting convenience.
- **Secure Storage:** Tamper-resistant locking keeps contents safe and discourages misuse.
- **Liners Made Easy:** While liners are not included as standard, you can easily add a plastic or steel liner (sold separately) for a customized waste management system.
- **Effortless Emptying:** The lift-off lid provides easy access for hassle-free emptying.
