The EnviroStack Stacking Recycling Bin is perfect for small offices looking to increase recycling rates. Available in 32 or 52 Litre capacity, its versatile design allows for customizable stacking to fit your needs. Its sleek design and WRAP color schemes make it easy for users to understand and minimize contamination.

These stacks can be used in conjuction with 32 Litre Upper Stack or Mid EnviroStack Bin to increase its capacity.

Does not include mid or upper bin components.
