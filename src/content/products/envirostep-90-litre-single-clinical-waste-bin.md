**Efficient Clinical Waste Management:** The EnviroStep Single Clinical Waste Bin is a robust 90-liter bin designed for effortless handling of high-volume medical waste. Built-in antimicrobial additives provide continuous protection against harmful bacteria, ensuring a hygienic environment. For user convenience, the bin features a front molded handle and non-marking wheels for smooth maneuvering. Easy maintenance is prioritized with a tilting sack frame that allows for quick and safe bin cleaning in one simple motion. Additionally, the minimal components enable complete disassembly for deep cleaning in under 8 minutes.
