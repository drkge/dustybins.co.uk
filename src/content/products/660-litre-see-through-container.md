**Clear Advantage: See-Through Recycling Bins!**

See-through recycling bins are designed for effortless dry mixed recyclables collection. Made with heavy-duty steel for ultimate durability, these bins feature strategically placed windows crafted from strong polycarbonate plastic. Choose from front, side, or even a combination of both window placements for maximum visibility.

**Benefits for Everyone:**

- **Easy Fill Level Check:** The transparent windows allow users to see exactly how full the bin is, preventing overflowing bins and the need to leave recyclables next to a full container.
- **Contamination Control:** Collection crews can easily spot any contamination in the recyclables through the windows, allowing them to address the issue before it affects the entire load.
- **Safety First:** The clear design ensures emptying crews can quickly identify anyone potentially trapped or sleeping inside the bin before emptying.
